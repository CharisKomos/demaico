Three practices, one delivery method. We embed with your team, work from your own data rather than a slide deck, and hand back a process your people can run without us in the room.

The Practices

What We
Do

From capability studies on a production line to the release pipeline behind your MES and the queues in your back office.

Lean Six Sigma Process Improvement 01

Process Capability & Variation Reduction
CTQ definition, measurement-system baselining, and capability studies (Cp, Cpk, sigma level) that turn "it feels unstable" into a number you can move — and prove you moved.
Lean Waste Elimination
Value-stream mapping and DOWNTIME waste classification pinpoint where time, material, and capacity leak out of the process — then we design the countermeasures and verify the recovery.
OEE & Throughput Improvement
Availability, performance, and quality broken down honestly, root-caused with Pareto and SPC, and improved with changes your operators actually keep after we leave.
Control & Sustained Gains
Control plans, continuous monitoring, and improvement-action tracking so the gain does not quietly erode once the project closes. Every action carries a before/after delta.

Manufacturing DevOps & Validation 02

CI/CD for Manufacturing Software
Version control, automated builds, and controlled release pipelines for MES, automation, and reporting software — so every change reaches production the same way, with the record to prove it.
Automated Testing
Regression and integration suites that run on every change, replacing manual re-testing and shrinking the qualification effort that sits behind each release.

Business Process Consulting 03

Operating Model & Process Design
We map how the work actually flows, remove the hand-offs and rework it has accumulated, and design a target process your people can run without us in the room.
KPI & Reporting Frameworks
A small set of measures that reflect what the business really needs to steer by, with the reporting and the data discipline to keep them honest.
Service Levels & Throughput
Queue, backlog, and cycle-time analysis for back-office and service operations — shortening lead times and steadying service levels without adding headcount.
Change Management & Training
Standard work, training, and a clean ownership handover, so the new way of working survives contact with a busy week.
