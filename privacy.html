Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · See also our Terms of Use

This Privacy Policy explains how DeMAICo ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects personal data when you visit demaico.com or contact us. We process personal data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, "GDPR") and the Cyprus Law Providing for the Protection of Natural Persons with Regard to the Processing of Personal Data (Law 125(I)/2018).

1. Data Controller

The controller responsible for your personal data is:

  • DeMAICo
  • Registered office: Panagioti Charalambous 9, 2109, Aglantzia, Nicosia
  • Email: privacy@demaico.com

2. What Data We Collect

a) Information you give us (contact form)

When you submit our contact form, we collect the data you choose to provide:

  • Your name, company, or organisation
  • Email address
  • The content of your responses to our questions (the challenge you want to solve, what you have already tried, and why now)
  • Any quick-pick options you select in the form and, if you used it, the figures and result from our sigma-level check

b) Technical data (hosting logs)

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare, which provides our DNS, content-delivery (CDN), and security layer. To serve the site and protect it against abuse, these providers may automatically process technical information such as your IP address, the pages you request, and your browser user-agent. We do not use this data to identify or profile you.

4. Cookies & Tracking

This website uses no analytics, advertising pixels, or third-party tracking technologies, and we set no cookies for marketing or profiling. Web fonts are self-hosted on our own domain, so no data is shared with font CDNs when you browse the site. Our content-delivery and security provider (Cloudflare) may set a single strictly necessary cookie to protect the site against automated abuse; such security cookies are essential to the service and are exempt from consent under the ePrivacy Directive. Because we set no non-essential cookies, no cookie consent banner is required.

Our processors are located in the United States. Where personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, the transfer is protected by appropriate safeguards under GDPR Chapter V — namely the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, together with the relevant provider's Data Processing Agreement.

7. How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain contact-form enquiries for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, after which they are deleted, unless a longer period is required to comply with a legal obligation or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

8. Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access — obtain a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten");
  • Restriction — limit how we process your data;
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format;
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@demaico.com. We will respond within one month.

9. Right to Lodge a Complaint

If you believe we have not handled your data lawfully, you may lodge a complaint with the Cyprus supervisory authority:

  • Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection (Cyprus)
  • Website: www.dataprotection.gov.cy

You may also complain to the supervisory authority in your own EU country of residence.

10. Data Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. The website is served exclusively over encrypted HTTPS.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent version. Material changes will be made prominent on this page.

12. Contact

For any question about this policy or your personal data, contact privacy@demaico.com.
