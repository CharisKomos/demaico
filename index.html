How We Work

Our
Engagement
Model

Every engagement runs the full DMAIC cycle — the same Lean Six Sigma methodology that has driven process improvement in manufacturing for decades, now applied to industrial software delivery.

D
Define
Scope the problem. Map your systems, data flows, CTQs, and compliance obligations — establishing what "good" looks like before any solution is proposed.
M
Measure
Baseline current performance. Connect historians and MES, establish KPIs, and quantify the gap between where your process is and where it needs to be.
A
Analyse
Root cause analysis, solution architecture, tooling choices, integration design, and a phased delivery plan with clear milestones and acceptance criteria.
I
Improve
Implementation, configuration, and testing — CI/CD pipelines, automation, integrations, and analytics built with your team. Every milestone demonstrated before we move forward.
C
Control
Handover, documentation, and team enablement. SPC monitoring to sustain the gains. Your team owns and operates the solution independently — with ongoing support if needed.
What We Do
01
CI/CD for DCS / SCADA
Every PLC and DCS configuration change is version-controlled, peer-reviewed, and deployed through an automated pipeline. MOC and PSM compliance checks run on every commit — eliminating manual checklists, closing audit gaps, and making rollbacks a one-click operation.
02
Simulation API Wrapping
Your Aspen Plus and HYSYS models become live services — callable by dashboards, schedulers, or any plant system via a simple API. Run what-if scenarios programmatically, integrate with your DCS or historian, and eliminate the manual back-and-forth of spreadsheet-driven simulation work.
03
Real-Time Simulation Feeding
Live PI Historian data flows directly into your simulation models — automatically cleaned, mapped, and delivered. Your simulations run on current plant conditions instead of yesterday's exports, giving you a continuously updated digital twin without building a new platform from scratch.
04
MES DevOps
MES changes that once dragged through months of informal processes move through structured, version-controlled pipelines. Every configuration change is reviewable, every deployment is repeatable, and rollbacks take minutes instead of days — dramatically compressing project timelines.
05
Automated Testing for MES
UAT cycles that consumed weeks of engineer time run overnight. Manual test scripts are replaced with automated test suites that execute consistently, catch regressions before they reach production, and produce documented evidence — turning the most painful part of every MES project into a non-event.
06
SAP–MES Integration Architecture
Production orders, material movements, and quality results flow reliably between SAP and your MES through a resilient message queue — not brittle point-to-point connections. One system going down no longer stalls the other, and the interfaces stop requiring constant engineer attention to stay operational.
07
Automated Batch Records
Batch records are compiled automatically at batch close — pulling from MES, PI Historian, and LIMS simultaneously — with deviations flagged in real time rather than discovered during QA review. What used to take days of manual data gathering is available the moment the batch ends, with a complete, audit-ready package. Built for pharmaceutical and regulated manufacturing environments.
DMAIC Analytics Platform

Continuous
Process
Intelligence

A purpose-built analytics engine that connects directly to your historian and MES — running the full DMAIC cycle continuously, not just at project milestones. CTQs are defined once; the platform measures, detects, classifies, and tracks improvement automatically, giving your team a live view of process health across every line and site.

01
Real-Time Measurement
Historian tags polled every 5 seconds against CTQ spec limits. Process capability (Cp, Cpk), OEE, and sigma level updated continuously — not in monthly reports.
02
Anomaly Detection
Rule-first ML engine: deterministic rules (priority ≥80) block ML inference. CUSUM, EWMA, and Western Electric SPC rules applied simultaneously to every tag.
03
Waste Classification
Every detected deviation is automatically classified against all 8 DOWNTIME waste categories — giving Lean teams an audit-ready evidence base without manual data gathering.
04
Improvement Tracking
Improvement actions logged with baseline and target sigma. Status advances open → in progress → verified → closed, with before/after sigma delta shown for every closed action.
05
GMP Audit Traceability
Every detection decision is source-attributed — rule name or ML model version, threshold, and timestamp. Compliant with 21 CFR Part 11 and EU GMP Annex 11 requirements.
06
Multi-Site Fleet View
Cross-project health dashboard surfaces the sites needing immediate attention — health score, critical anomaly count, SPC violations, and top waste category in a single triage view.
