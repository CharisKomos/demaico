How We Work

Our
Engagement
Model

Every engagement runs the same DMAIC cycle — the discipline at the heart of Six Sigma Lean Production. It is our delivery framework for all consulting and engineering work: a structured method that scopes, measures, analyses, improves, and sustains every change we make to your process.

D
Define
We scope the engagement with you — getting to know the process, its customers, and what they truly value. We agree on the problem, the goals, and exactly what "better" looks like before we propose a single change.
M
Measure
We baseline the current state. We pull data from the systems you already use and measure how the process really performs today — how capable it is, how fast it runs, and how often it falls short. It's the honest starting point we work from.
A
Analyse
We find the causes that actually matter. We separate the real drivers from the noise and trace every issue back to the waste or variation behind it — building the evidence for what to fix, and what to leave alone.
I
Improve
We design and deliver the fix — new ways of working, process changes, and any supporting tools or automation. We set a baseline and a target for each improvement, pilot it, and prove it works before it's accepted.
C
Control
We lock in the gains. We put the right controls, routines, and ongoing monitoring in place to keep the process on target — catching drift early and tracking every action from open to verified to closed, with a clear before-and-after. Then your team owns the result.
Our Core Practice

Six Sigma
Lean Production
Consulting

We embed with your team and drive measurable process improvement — reducing variation, eliminating waste, and raising process capability across every line and site. Consulting and hands-on engineering, delivered through the DMAIC cycle and evidenced with your own data, not a slide deck.

OEE & Throughput Improvement
Availability, performance, and quality broken down honestly, root-caused with Pareto and SPC, and improved with changes your operators actually keep after we leave.
Control & Sustained Gains
Control plans, continuous monitoring, and improvement-action tracking so the sigma you gained does not quietly erode once the project closes. Every action carries a before/after delta.
The Engine Behind Our Consultancy

Our DMAIC
Analytics
Platform

Where a typical Six Sigma project produces a one-off report, ours produces a living instrument. CTQs are defined once; the platform then measures, detects, classifies, and tracks improvement automatically — giving your team a continuous view of process health long after the engagement ends.

  • An in-house tool, used on engagements — never licensed or handed over as a product.
  • Works on both real-time and batch data — scoring live streams as they arrive and back-loading historical exports just the same.
  • Every detection is source-attributed — rule or model, threshold, and timestamp — for GMP-grade traceability.
  • Selected screens and reports become part of your project deliverables and control plan.
01
Real-Time Measurement
Historian tags scored against CTQ spec limits every 5 seconds. Process capability (Cp, Cpk), OEE, and sigma level updated continuously — not in a monthly report.
02
Anomaly Detection
Rule-first ML engine: deterministic rules (priority ≥80) take precedence over ML inference. CUSUM, EWMA, and Western Electric SPC rules applied simultaneously to every tag.
03
Waste Classification
Every detected deviation is automatically classified against all 8 DOWNTIME waste categories — giving Lean teams an audit-ready evidence base without manual data gathering.
04
Improvement Tracking
Improvement actions logged with baseline and target sigma. Status advances open → in progress → verified → closed, with the before/after sigma delta shown for every closed action.
05
GMP Audit Traceability
Every detection decision is source-attributed — rule name or ML model version, threshold, and timestamp. Aligned with 21 CFR Part 11 and EU GMP Annex 11 requirements.
06
Multi-Site Fleet View
A cross-project health dashboard surfaces the sites needing attention first — health score, critical anomaly count, SPC violations, and top waste category in a single triage view.
