How We Work

Our
Engagement
Model

Every engagement runs the same DMAIC cycle. It is our delivery framework for all consulting and engineering work: a structured method that scopes, measures, analyses, improves, and sustains every change we make — whether the process is a production line or a back-office queue.

D
Define
We scope the engagement with you — getting to know the process, its customers, and what they truly value. We agree on the problem, the goals, and exactly what "better" looks like before we propose a single change.
M
Measure
We baseline the current state. We pull data from the systems you already use and measure how the process really performs today — how capable it is, how fast it runs, and how often it falls short. It's the honest starting point we work from.
A
Analyse
We find the causes that actually matter. We separate the real drivers from the noise and trace every issue back to the waste or variation behind it — building the evidence for what to fix, and what to leave alone.
I
Improve
We design and deliver the fix — new ways of working, process changes, and any supporting tools or automation. We set a baseline and a target for each improvement, pilot it, and prove it works before it's accepted.
C
Control
We lock in the gains. We put the right controls, routines, and ongoing monitoring in place to keep the process on target — catching drift early and tracking every action from open to verified to closed, with a clear before-and-after. Then your team owns the result.
