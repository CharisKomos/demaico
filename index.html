How We Work

Our
Engagement
Model

Every engagement follows the same structured approach — from understanding your environment to handing over a solution your team can own and operate independently.

1
Discover
We map your current systems, data flows, pain points, and constraints — including compliance obligations and integration dependencies — before any solution is proposed.
2
Design
We produce a solution architecture with integration points, tooling choices, security boundaries, and a phased delivery plan with clear milestones and acceptance criteria.

Handover

What We Do
01
CI/CD for DCS / SCADA
Every PLC and DCS configuration change is version-controlled, peer-reviewed, and deployed through an automated pipeline. MOC and PSM compliance checks run on every commit — eliminating manual checklists, closing audit gaps, and making rollbacks a one-click operation.
02
Simulation API Wrapping
Your Aspen Plus and HYSYS models become live services — callable by dashboards, schedulers, or any plant system via a simple API. Run what-if scenarios programmatically, integrate with your DCS or historian, and eliminate the manual back-and-forth of spreadsheet-driven simulation work.
03
Real-Time Simulation Feeding
Live PI Historian data flows directly into your simulation models — automatically cleaned, mapped, and delivered. Your simulations run on current plant conditions instead of yesterday's exports, giving you a continuously updated digital twin without building a new platform from scratch.
04
MES DevOps
MES changes that once dragged through months of informal processes move through structured, version-controlled pipelines. Every configuration change is reviewable, every deployment is repeatable, and rollbacks take minutes instead of days — dramatically compressing project timelines.
05
Automated Testing for MES
UAT cycles that consumed weeks of engineer time run overnight. Manual test scripts are replaced with automated test suites that execute consistently, catch regressions before they reach production, and produce documented evidence — turning the most painful part of every MES project into a non-event.
06
SAP–MES Integration Architecture
Production orders, material movements, and quality results flow reliably between SAP and your MES through a resilient message queue — not brittle point-to-point connections. One system going down no longer stalls the other, and the interfaces stop requiring constant engineer attention to stay operational.
07
Automated Batch Records
Batch records are compiled automatically at batch close — pulling from MES, PI Historian, and LIMS simultaneously — with deviations flagged in real time rather than discovered during QA review. What used to take days of manual data gathering is available the moment the batch ends, with a complete, audit-ready package. Built for pharmaceutical and regulated manufacturing environments.
